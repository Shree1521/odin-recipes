Pesto Recipe

Ingredients

  • Basil - 600g
  • Pine nuts- 300g
  • Garlic cloves - 18 cloves or approx 1.5 - 2 bulbs
  • Lemons x 4
  • Parmesan cheese - 300g
  • Salt - 2 teaspoons
  • Black pepper - 160 x cracks
  • Extra virgin olive oil - 400ml

Method

  1. Toast the pine nuts in a pan until slightly brown.
  2. Once brown, remove from pan into bowl so they don't continue to cook.
  3. Grate all the parmesan into a food processor.
  4. Juice all the lemons into the food processor.
  5. Add the pine nuts into the food processor and blitz until smooth.
  6. Peel the garlic cloves, cut the ends off the basil bunches and wash all of it.
  7. To the food processor add the basil, garlic, oil, salt and pepper.
  8. Blitz until smooth.
