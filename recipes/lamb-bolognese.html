Lamb Bolognese Recipe

Ingredients

  • White Onions x 3
  • Celery x 3 or 4 (approx similar amount of carrot)
  • Carrots x 4
  • 800g mince meat
  • Dried arbol chillies x 8
  • Garlic cloves x 8 (crushed)
  • Anchovies x 1 pack
  • Tomato paste x 1 jar 180g
  • Red wine x 150ml
  • Chicken stock (500ml)
  • Basil (small bunch)
  • Oregano (small bunch)
  • Rosemary (small bunch)
  • Bay leaves x 2
  • Cooking string
  • Parmesan cheese + 2 x rinds

Method

  1. Dice up the onions, celery and carrots (very small!). We are looking for a ratio of 2:1:1 ratio of onions:celery:carrots.
  2. In a pan, heat some olive oil and add the dried chilis.
  3. Cook until you can smell them and remove from oil.
  4. Brown off the meat in the oil.
  5. Remove the meat into the sieve over a bowl, and use some of the juice to cook the sauce.
  6. Add the onions, garlic, carrots and celery, cook it until it's softened (10-12 mins).
  7. Dice up the anchovies and add these.
  8. Add the tomato paste & cook it down until you get sticky bits on the pan.
  9. Add the red wine to deglaze and cook until the alcohol has burned off.
  10. Add the meat back in and add chicken stock.
  11. Wash and tie up a couple of springs of basil, oregano, rosemary, and 2 x bay leaves (this is called a bouquet garni).
  12. Add the bouquet garni into the pot along with the parmesan rinds.
  13. Cook for 1 hour on a low simmer, then remove the rinds and garni.
  14. Cut up a couple of sprigs of fresh basil and cut up 2 of the red chillies from earlier. Add these into the pot.
  15. Serve with pasta and grate some parmesan over and a few cracks of black pepper before you eat.
