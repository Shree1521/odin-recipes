Red Lentil Soup

Ingredients

  • Extra virgin olive oil - 4 tablespoons.
  • White onions - x2 medium, peeled and diced.
  • Garlic - x6 cloves, crushed.
  • Carrots - x4 to 6, peeled and diced.
  • Potatoes - x2 medium, peeled and diced.
  • Tomato puree - x4 tablespoons, heaped.
  • Aleppo pepper - x4 teaspoons.
  • Salt - x1 teaspoon.
  • Cumin powder - x2 teaspoons.
  • Coriander powder - 1x teaspoon.
  • Red lenils - 2 cups, washed.
  • Chicken stock or broth - 3L

Method

  1. In a large pan, heat using mercury ball technique, then add oil and onions.
  2. Cook onions until softened.
  3. Add the garlic, aleppo pepper, salt, cumin powder, coriander powder, carrots and potatoes and cook for 5-7 mins.
  4. Add the lentils and chicken stock/broth, bring to boil.
  5. Boil for 4 - 5 mins, taste it to check salt levels, then lower heat and cover with lid. Leave a small opening.
  6. Simmer on lowest heat setting for 15-20 mins. Stir every 5 mins.
  7. Taste again to check salt levels. If it's good, then it is now ready to blend.
  8. Serve with a garnish of fresh coriander.
